# UNDO — Progression

## Target

Approximate total playtime: 4–6 hours.

Five-act structure with sixteen major puzzle spaces.

## Mechanical progression

| Range | Primary concept |
|---|---|
| 0–20 min | single-state suppression |
| 20–45 min | NPC exploitation |
| 45–75 min | Consequence Persistence |
| 75–120 min | suppression release |
| 2–3 h | NPC reactions |
| 3–4 h | two simultaneous corrections |
| 4 h+ | masked events / causal forks |
| final | paradox construction and narrative limit |

## Suppression capacity

- Acts 1–2: 1
- Act 3 onward: 2
- Late Act 4 / Act 5: up to 3

No capacity beyond 3 is currently planned.

## Act I — DISCOVERY

Purpose: teach the fundamental rule.

### L01 Closing Time
NPC closes a door; player suppresses the close event.

### L02 After You
An employee crosses a door; suppressing its later closure lets the player follow.

### L03 The Light
A worker turns off lighting; suppressing that event reactivates a sensor/system, teaching that the world reacts.

## Act II — CONTROL

Purpose: begin manipulating routines rather than individual obstacles.

### L04 Patrol
Change a door/lock condition to redirect a guard.

### L05 Missing
Badge is taken, scanned, and used. Suppress the take event after the useful scan consequence persists.

### L06 Routine
Two workers and document-transfer systems demonstrate accumulation of independent events.

### L07 Let It Close
First release-timing puzzle. Maintain an open state, allow an NPC through, then release to induce an emergency reaction.

## Act III — CONSEQUENCE

Purpose: introduce machinery, contradiction reactions, and a second maintained suppression.

### L08 Restoration
Conveyor/scanner/box systems teach State Preparation.

### L09 Inspection
Abnormal machine state causes maintenance reaction.

### L10 Two Errors
A situation cannot be solved with one maintained correction. Second capacity becomes available naturally through the correction system.

### L11 Crossed Paths
Two corrections alter two actors' routes until their paths create a new useful event.

## Act IV — ARCHIVE

Purpose: deepen known rules instead of adding unrelated mechanics.

### L12 Overwritten
Multiple historical mutations affect the same channel; introduces masked events.

### L13 Audit
Authorization is suppressed after a person has already entered, causing security to react to a person now considered unauthorized.

### L14 Contradiction
Large multi-space puzzle requiring a deliberately impossible combination of door, archive, guard, alarm, and authorization states.

## Act V — RECORD 0

Purpose: use full mechanical mastery for narrative reconstruction and conclusion.

### L15 Reconstruction
Player attempts to change multiple parts of the historical accident. Three corrections can approach a seemingly solved state, but the decisive 39-second gap contains no suppressible event.

### L16 Record 0
Final use of the complete ruleset. The present-day record destruction procedure becomes an event the player can manipulate.

## Rhythm

Do not chain puzzles without relief.

Target rhythm:
- 15–25 min active puzzle solving,
- short traversal/environmental narrative segment,
- next puzzle.

Include non-puzzle breathing spaces such as:
- break room,
- copy room,
- archive corridor,
- stairs,
- elevator,
- maintenance office.

## Teaching model

Each space should primarily function as one of:
- Teach,
- Test,
- Combine,
- Twist.

Do not increase difficulty only by increasing the number of objects.
