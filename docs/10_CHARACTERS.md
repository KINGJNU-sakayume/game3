# UNDO — Characters

## Han Jaemin (한재민)

### Present
- Age: 43
- Role: records-integrity reviewer / verification staff
- Repository 4 employee for approximately 17 years
- Present game occurs during his final night shift before reassignment/closure transition

### At the accident
- Age: 27
- Approximately one year into employment

### Character

Jaemin is:
- careful,
- procedural,
- reliable,
- slow to make irreversible decisions,
- reserved but not antisocial,
- more comfortable verifying than improvising.

His defining trait is not cowardice. It is a habit of checking one more time before acting.

After the historical accident, small habits become more pronounced:
- rechecking alarms,
- confirming locks,
- reviewing status indicators,
- personally verifying tasks marked complete.

Do not diagnose or label him through exposition.

### Dialogue

Jaemin is quiet but not literally mute.

Target total voiced/spoken lines across the game should remain low. Reactions are short and situational.

Avoid explicit emotional explanation such as:
- "It was all my fault."
- "I have regretted this for sixteen years."
- "Now I forgive myself."

## Yoon Seojin (윤서진)

### At the accident
- Age: 31
- Role: records-restoration technician
- Several years more experienced at Repository 4 than Jaemin

### Character

Seojin is:
- practical,
- fast-working,
- technically skilled,
- socially easier than Jaemin,
- somewhat impatient with obsolete procedure,
- serious about the purpose of her work without worshipping process.

She is not idealized after death.

Environmental traces should show ordinary imperfections:
- delayed paperwork,
- jokes,
- handwritten reminders,
- occasional shortcuts,
- small disagreements,
- normal collegial behavior.

### Relationship with Jaemin

They were colleagues with genuine everyday familiarity, not lovers and not family.

They:
- shared occasional meals during late shifts,
- joked,
- helped each other with work,
- disagreed mildly about procedure.

The game is intentionally not a tragic romance.

## Seojin's handwriting

A recurring environmental signature is Seojin's handwritten equipment notes:
- filter reminders,
- warnings about a sticky mechanism,
- small maintenance comments,
- brief jokes.

The player encounters the handwriting before fully understanding who she was.

## Historical accident

Seojin enters B3 Restoration Room 3 to inspect what initially appears to be a routine environmental-control fault.

A compound failure follows:
- power-control anomaly,
- restoration equipment shutdown,
- environmental-control fault,
- protective isolation,
- locked electronic door,
- degraded communication,
- emergency-release path failure.

The event is initially ambiguous rather than obviously lethal.

## Critical communication

Seojin's important request is deliberately ordinary in tone:

> "재민 씨, 이거 제어실에서 한번만 봐줄래요?"

The line should not become melodramatic foreshadowing.

Her final communications remain fragmentary and mundane. Do not create a posthumous farewell recording.

## Official responsibility

The investigation identifies multiple design/procedure failures and notes delayed emergency action, but does not establish a clear procedural violation or legal responsibility for Jaemin.

It also cannot establish that an immediate response would certainly have saved Seojin.

This uncertainty is essential.
